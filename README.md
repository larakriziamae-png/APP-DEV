# My Favorite Series: Stranger Things

## About the Series
Stranger Things is a science fiction and mystery series about a group of friends who discover strange events happening in their town.

## Why I Like It
I like this series because it has an interesting story, exciting adventures, and unexpected twists. It also teaches the importance of friendship, courage, and teamwork.
